Questions

Good to know before we talk.

What does the free IT audit include?

A review of your devices, user accounts, backups, email security and network, followed by a clear written summary of risks and recommended next steps. There is no obligation to continue with us.

Do you offer fixed monthly pricing?

Yes. Our managed IT plans are priced per user or device each month, so you can budget with confidence.

Can you help us move to Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace?

Yes. We plan and run the migration of email, files and applications, and train your team so the change is smooth.

How quickly do you respond to issues?

Our helpdesk is available around the clock for monitored systems, and urgent issues are prioritised first.
